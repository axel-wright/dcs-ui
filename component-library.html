Introduction

Establishing proper machine alignment is the foundation of high-precision CNC manufacturing. In this study, we look at milling custom brackets for our dust collection manifolds using 3/4-inch stock. A structured approach ensures consistent tolerances and prevents waste.

Material Selection

For these brackets, we selected 6061-T6 aluminum due to its exceptional strength-to-weight ratio and clean machinability. When sourcing billet stock, ensuring thickness tolerances are verified prevents Z-axis discrepancies on critical pockets.

Tool Path Strategy

We ran adaptive clearing operations using a 3-flute carbide end mill. Feeds and speeds were locked at 8,200 RPM and 72 IPM, maintaining a consistent radial engagement to minimize heat build-up. Stepdowns were set to 0.40 inches.

Results & Finish

Final contour passes left a pristine finish requiring minimal post-processing. Verification measurements confirm bracket bore sizes are within our targeted ±0.002-inch tolerance. Clean anodizing adds protection and matches our workshop color scheme.
